# Cyclops Thresholders: art to generate

Six images replace the Kingmaker Thresholder art. Save each file at the path shown; the actor sources already reference these paths.

Generate all six with Image Gen 2.5 Sunburst.

## Shared style

High-end fantasy concept art with the production value of a feature-film creature design: photoreal materials with detailed textures, cinematic rim and key lighting, sharp focus, and grounded anatomy. No scenery or text. The subject is a cyclops: a Large humanoid giant, about twelve feet tall, broad-shouldered and heavily muscled, with one large central eye socket above a wide nose. Every Thresholder has surrendered their sight to Yog-Sothoth, so the single socket is empty, sealed, or bound shut, and a pale spiral scar is carved into the brow or scalp. The palette uses deep indigo and violet cloth against weathered grey-olive or ruddy cyclops skin.

## Token frame

Tokens must be square (1:1) with a transparent background. Frame a head-and-torso crop inside a round token ring of dark bronze with a worn gold inner rim and a black interior, matching the Kingmaker token set. Let hands, weapons, or cloth break past the ring's edge.
